# NSW UCPR approved forms

The published forms themselves, used as the template for court document
precedents rather than a layout reconstructed in code.

## Why they are here

A court document is filed under a prescribed form. Rebuilding that layout by
hand — which is what `lib/precedents/courtFormDocx.ts` does — gets the words
right and the document wrong: the real Form 3A is 12 tables and 77 rows, and
a reconstruction drifts from it every time the form is amended. Filling the
published file instead means the layout is the official one by construction.

## Provenance

Downloaded from the NSW Department of Communities and Justice UCPR forms page:
<https://www.dcj.nsw.gov.au/content/dcj/ctsd/ucpr/ucpr.html>

| File | Form | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `ucpr_form_3a_v7.docx` | Statement of claim (legally represented) | UCPR 6.2 |
| `ucpr_form_4a_v4.docx` | Summons (legally represented) | UCPR 6.4 |
| `ucpr_form_6a_v1.docx` | Appearance | UCPR 6.9, 6.11 |
| `ucpr_form_7a_v5.docx` | Defence (legally represented) | UCPR 14.3 |
| `ucpr_form_9_v6.docx` | Statement of cross-claim | UCPR 9.1 |
| `ucpr_form_11_v3.docx` | List of documents | UCPR 21.3 |
| `ucpr_form_19_v2.docx` | Notice to produce for inspection | UCPR 21.10 |
| `ucpr_form_26a_v5.docx` | Subpoena to produce | UCPR 33.2 |
| `ucpr_form_40_v8.docx` | Affidavit | UCPR 35.1 |

Every one except Form 40 is published as a Word 97-2003 `.doc`. Those were
converted once to `.docx` through the LibreOffice service in `docservice/`
(deployed at `diract-docservice.fly.dev`), because conversion takes about 26
seconds and cannot sit in the path of a user waiting for a download. macOS
`textutil` is not a substitute — it flattens every table.

## Keeping them current

Approved forms are amended, and the version is part of the form. When one
changes: download the new file, convert it the same way, replace the file
here, and update the version in the table above **and** in the precedent's
`reviewNote` in `lib/precedents/library/courtNswUcpr.ts`.
